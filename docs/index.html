    About this map

    Each step of the slider shows where the coastline sat at that time, from 100,000 years ago to the present. Shorelines are contours of the GEBCO seabed elevation grid drawn at the sea level of each age.

    Sea level combines the global curve of Spratt & Lisiecki (2016) with Bonaparte Gulf data (Yokoyama et al. 2000; Ishiwa et al. 2019) for 11–27 ka. The seabed has changed since then through sediment, reef growth and subsidence, so treat the shorelines as approximate.

    Faunal lines (Wallace, Huxley, Weber, Lydekker) are schematic traces after standard published maps, not surveyed boundaries.

    Keys: ◀ ▶ step through time, Space plays and pauses. Measure: click points on the map for distances in km, Esc to finish.
