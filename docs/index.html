About this map

Each step of the slider shows where the coast from Java to New Guinea sat at that time, from 100,000 years ago to the present. Shorelines are contours of the GEBCO seabed elevation grid drawn at the sea level of each age.

Sea level combines the global curve of Spratt & Lisiecki (2016) with Bonaparte Gulf data (Yokoyama et al. 2000; Ishiwa et al. 2019) for 11–27 ka. The seabed has changed since then through sediment, reef growth and subsidence, so treat the shorelines as approximate.

Keys: ◀ ▶ step through time, Space plays and pauses.
